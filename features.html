Inventory

One card per part. Everything you'd want on it.

Each item gets an auto ID (C-0001 for components, F- filament, P- PCBs, S- screws), a photo, its drawer, unit price, supplier part code and a stock counter you can nudge with the plus and minus buttons.

  • Five live stats: items, total units, low stock, out of stock, inventory value
  • Search by name, ID, location or barcode; sort and filter by status
  • Packs and units. A bag of 100 counts as 1 pack and 100 units, so "1.84 packs · 184 units" is exactly right
  • Low and out-of-stock banners on the card, a per-item threshold, and optional in-app notifications
  • Filament is first-class: material, diameter, colour swatch, spool weight
  • Reorder in one click. The green Buy button opens the supplier page you saved on the part
  • Compact list view (new in V4): one dense row per part with the same steppers and a one-click reorder button

Compartments

Map the real storage. Then never lose a part again.

Add a storage unit and say what it is: a drawer cabinet, a shelf, a single drawer, a box, a bin, a bag, a rack, or your own word. In advanced mode Parts Bin draws it as a grid and you pick the exact position when you store a part, so its card reads Drawer cabinet · #18. In simple mode a part is just "in the bag", which is right for loose bags where a position can't be tracked.

  • Small, medium or large presets, or your own rows by columns, laid out horizontally (slabs) or vertically (spools standing on a shelf)
  • A part's Location is its compartment. Make a new one right from the item form
  • Sync with compartments turns your old plain-text locations into compartments in one click and moves the parts in
  • Big units show the first few parts and a Show all button that opens the full list with a filter

Orders

An order holds several products with quantities, prices, photos, original IDs, buy links and the compartment each one goes to, plus a tracking number and expected date. Import a parts list straight into an order and each line tells you whether it's new or already in your library. When the box arrives, mark it and every line is added to inventory or merged into the existing part by its original ID. Turn on auto-receive and it happens on the expected date by itself. Supplier prices in another currency convert to yours with one click.

Projects

Give a project a description, photos and a checklist with a progress bar. Allocate the parts it needs and they're taken out of stock; delete the project and they go back. Each card shows the project's total parts cost. Finished projects can auto-archive.

New in V4

Parts list in. Order-ready file out.

Export the parts list (BOM) from your PCB design tool and pick it in Parts Bin. Original IDs, quantities, prices and links are read from the file; each row takes a photo (or fetches one from the buy link), a low-stock alert and a compartment, and tells you if that part is already in your library. Done adds them all. Or drop them into an Order instead, so they only count once they arrive. Supplier prices in another currency convert to yours with one click.

  • Import .xlsx or .csv
  • Export an order-ready CSV keyed on the supplier part code, ready to upload to your board house
  • Or a full CSV of every field for spreadsheets and backups
  • Scope: the current filtered view or the whole library. Items with no part code are counted and reported, never silently dropped
  • Exports are read-only. They never change your data

New in V4

The assistant: an AI that can see your inventory and change it.

Open the Assistant tab and chat. It knows every item in your inventory, so it answers from what's actually on your bench. Ask what you're low on, what you can build right now, or what a project idea would still need.

  • Suggest projects buildable from your stock, each with the parts it uses. Recommend parts to round out what you have
  • Create and edit items (name, quantity, drawer, price, link), create and edit projects, and add checklist steps, all from a sentence
  • Apply, don't assume. It never changes anything by itself. Each change shows as a button; tap Apply (or turn on auto-apply) and every change is undoable
  • Memory. It remembers lasting facts about you, your tools and suppliers on its own, and you can give it standing instructions on how to behave
  • Slash commands for the common asks: /projects /parts /low /explain /set
  • Your calendar too. It can see your daily tasks and events, schedule a task for next Tuesday or add an event, unless you switch that off in Settings, in which case it is never shown the calendar at all
  • Multiple chats, replies format as they type, and it can read them aloud
  • Your choice of model. Bring your own key from a cloud AI provider (stored only on your PC), or run a local model on your machine so nothing leaves it

The assistant is optional and off until you set it up in Settings. Everything else in Parts Bin works without it.

New in V4

Daily tasks, with a calendar behind them.

The tasks button up top opens a planner for what you're doing on the bench today. Flip to the Calendar tab and you can put tasks on future days: pick next Tuesday, type "reflow the boards", "print the lid", and on Tuesday they are simply there in your list. Tick repeat every week and ticking one off books next week's copy, which is how you build a routine.

  • Events are separate. An event has a time, and Parts Bin tells you when it comes up: an in-app banner, and a Windows notification even when the app is minimised
  • Unticked tasks carry over to the next day with a note of when they were due, or tick Delete after day ends to start every day clean
  • The assistant can see it and schedule tasks or add events for you, unless you turn that off in Settings, in which case it is never shown the calendar at all
  • Finish every task for the day and there is a little confetti. That has an off switch too

New in V4

Compact list: the whole bench on one screen.

Cards are lovely when you want the photo. When you want fifty parts in view at once, switch Settings, Appearance, Inventory view to the compact list: one dense row per part with the Part ID, the supplier's original ID, where it lives, the price and a live stock counter. The same search, filters and sort apply, and the row opens the part.

  • Two IDs side by side: Parts Bin's own C-0031 and the original catalogue ID, never mixed up
  • Low and out-of-stock rows are outlined, so a scan down the list finds them
  • Works with every theme and style preset; switch back to cards any time

NEW IN V4

Filter and sort

Narrow the list by original ID, stock state (in, low, out) and compartment, on top of search, and sort by name, ID, quantity or value. One click clears it and the toolbar shows "12 of 40".

Print labels

Barcode labels in extra small, small, medium or large for drawers, bags and spools. Select a few items, or the whole view, and hit Print labels.

Scan to find

Point your webcam at a label (or type its code) and Parts Bin shows the part, where it lives and how many you have, with buttons to add stock, open it, or scan the next one. An unknown code offers to become a new item. Scanning runs on your PC, not in the cloud.

NEW IN V4

Calculator

A floating calculator that does maths and live currency conversion, so you can price an order without leaving the app.

NEW IN V4

Custom tabs

Add your own sidebar tabs, each with an image and its own ID prefix (Stencils becomes T-0001). Hide any built-in tab you don't use; items stay in the library and still show under All.

NEW IN V4

Settings in tabs

General, Appearance, Sidebar tabs, Materials and locations, AI assistant, Notifications, Data and backup, Updates. Your name and greeting, your currency and the currency your suppliers use, default low-stock threshold, density, confetti, resizable sidebar, and the app version.

9 style presets. 16 themes. Your accent, your font.

Presets rebuild the whole app: fonts, edges, backgrounds. Themes recolour it. Pick a base, override the accent with any hex colour, change the font, and choose comfortable or compact density. Hover a preset to see its colours on this site.

The 16 colour themes: Dark, Light, Paper, Snow, Mint, Sakura, Midnight, Ocean, Forest, Rose, Amber, Grape, Dracula, Nord, Sunset, Cyber.

Privacy and backups

Your data never leaves your PC.

There is no server. There is no account. Parts Bin can't upload your inventory because there's nowhere for it to go.

Saved on this PC

V4 stores everything in the app's own private storage. It survives restarts and updates.

Recovery snapshot

Every save also writes a hidden recovery copy. If the main store ever comes back empty, the snapshot restores itself.

Backup to file

Settings, Data and backup, Backup to file. Move your inventory to another PC or keep a spare copy. Restore from the same screen.

PIN lock

An optional 4-digit PIN, with "require when the app opens". A casual lock so others can't just open it. Stored on this PC only.

Updates when you want them

V4 never checks for updates on its own. Settings, Updates has a button that opens the Releases page in your browser. Run the new installer and your data isn't touched.

Open source

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript inside a small native shell. Read every line on GitHub.
